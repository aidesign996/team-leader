# Changelog

## 0.5.17 — 2026-09-09

Research, assessments, reports, and ongoing services can use the same six responsibilities, mapped to the deliverable. Routine work reuses adopted methods instead of restarting method design for every request.

Review of a knowledge deliverable now explicitly checks what its intended reader can understand, judge, or do, including the decisive reasons and limits. Source accuracy, chapter coverage, and formatting support this use check; they do not replace it.

Existing design exploration and faithful implementation, complete-team setup with task-specific activation, permissions, project-scoped experience, and continuation remain in place. No runtime dependency or model switch is added.

### Verification and limits

The maintainer reports independent semantic review across 12 cases and two isolated forward-delivery rounds. The publisher verified the exact 22-file source package, ZIP, manifest, and installed 21 Skill files. The package includes the MIT license. These checks do not establish long-term autonomous coordination, adoption in every project, quota savings, or maximum model performance. No Astra/Sol switch comparison was performed.

## 0.5.16 — 2026-09-07

Team Leader now has a dedicated public repository. This release contains the same 22-file MIT package previously published in ai-practice; no Skill behavior changes are introduced by this move.

In 0.5.16, choosing and authorizing team mode establishes six responsibilities, then activates the roles needed for each task. Solo work and existing teams remain supported. Package integrity and clean-directory extraction have been checked; broader outcome and usage-saving claims remain unverified.

English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese introductions share one public feedback discussion. Existing ai-practice download links remain available. Future public Team Leader source and releases will be maintained here.
